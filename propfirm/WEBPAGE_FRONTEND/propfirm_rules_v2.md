## 4. That's It — 5 Rules to Remember

1. **Hit the profit target.**
2. **Don't lose more than 4% in a single day** — and no single loss can eat more than 60% of that (i.e. 2.4%) in one go.
3. **Don't lose more than 8% overall.**
4. **Trade on at least [5–6] different days** — no one-shot passes.
5. **No single day should make more than 30% of your total profit** — consistency, not luck.

Everything else — position sizing, holding overnight, how many trades you take — is entirely up to you.

---

## 5. Why This Is Harder Than It Looks

Four numbers and a "trade a few days" rule feel almost too simple — which is exactly why most traders underestimate it. In practice:

- **The 4% daily cap is tighter than most traders' actual day-to-day swings.** One bad NIFTY option trade can eat it in minutes if you're not managing size.
- **The 2.4% single-loss cap closes the obvious workaround.** Without it, a trader could stay "inside" the 4% by taking one oversized swing that burns almost the whole daily allowance in a single trade. This forces losses to actually be spread and managed, not just numerically under the ceiling.
- **The 30% consistency rule quietly kills the lucky trade.** You can hit your target in one big win and still fail, because that single day carried too much of the total. It forces you to be *repeatably* good, not just right once.
- **The minimum trading days rule removes the shortcut of gambling it in a single session.** You have to survive your own risk management over multiple days, not just get lucky once.

None of this needs to be spelled out in scary language on the page — the numbers do the filtering by themselves. Traders who read "10%, 4%, 8%, 6 days" and think "easy" are exactly the ones who come back three days later understanding why it isn't.

### 6.1 Average Daily Profit Method

Instead of paying the raw total profit from a cycle, the payout is smoothed to reward consistency over lucky single days:

1. **Average Daily Profit = Total Cycle Profit ÷ Number of Trading Days**
2. **Per-day cap = 2× Average Daily Profit.** Any single day's actual profit above this cap only counts up to the cap for this cycle's payout
3. **Excess above the cap is not lost** — it rolls forward and gets added into the next cycle's profit total, where it goes through the same averaging process again

**Worked example (5-day cycle, ₹20,000 total profit):**

| Day | Actual Profit | Counted This Cycle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₹2,000 | ₹2,000 |
| 2 | ₹1,000 | ₹1,000 |
| 3 | ₹16,000 | ₹8,000 (capped at 2× avg) |
| 4 | ₹500 | ₹500 |
| 5 | ₹500 | ₹500 |

Average = ₹4,000/day → cap = ₹8,000/day → **Payout this cycle = ₹12,000** (the remaining ₹8,000 from Day 3 rolls into next cycle).

This rewards traders who profit steadily across days and slows down (never fully blocks) payouts to traders whose profit came from one outsized swing.
